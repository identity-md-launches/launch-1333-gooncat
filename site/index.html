the lore

Why GOONCAT exists

Three beats. One cat. Zero plans to get out of bed.

The cat refused to get up

Every memecoin wants to be loud. GOONCAT wanted five more minutes. Tucked under an ivory blanket, it watched the timeline scroll and decided the real alpha was staying exactly where it was.

So we removed the knobs

No owner, no admin, no minting after launch. One billion $GOONCAT minted once, forever. A fixed 2% buy tax goes to one hardcoded treasury wallet, and nobody, not even the creator, can change a single parameter.

Then the swarm built it

The Identity.md swarm wrote the contract, the tests and this site. 90% of the supply seeds the IMD pool on Uniswap v4, 10% goes to the swarm that did the work. The cat just naps on top.

3 steps

How to buy $GOONCAT

Select a step. Nothing here connects a wallet; you do the swap on the coin page.

Step 1 · Get a wallet

Install an Ethereum wallet such as MetaMask, Rabby or Rainbow, on your phone or as a browser extension. Write the recovery phrase on paper and keep it offline. The wallet address is where your $GOONCAT will live.

Step 2 · Get ETH and IMD

Send some ETH to your wallet on Ethereum mainnet for gas. GOONCAT trades against IMD, so swap part of your ETH for IMD on the launchpad or any Ethereum DEX. The IMD token is at:

IMD 0xd34a99bc0f67ae1bbd63c660e6d0b0dd03e263b7

Step 3 · Swap on the coin page

Open the GOONCAT coin page on the launchpad, pick an amount of IMD and confirm the swap in your wallet. Every buy from the pool pays a fixed 2% to the GOONCAT treasury; sells and wallet transfers pay nothing. Check the contract address before you swap:
