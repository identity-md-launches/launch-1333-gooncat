# GOONCAT image-generation prompts

Tool: built-in Codex `image_gen` (five separate calls, one image per approach).
Every call used the creator's supplied photograph as a reference with an opaque background.
The generated originals were 1254 × 1254 RGB PNGs; delivered copies were resampled to
1024 × 1024 without redrawing, recoloring, cropping or compositing the artwork.

## Option 1

Use case: logo-brand. Create one 1024x1024 square opaque GOONCAT token mascot logo. The input image is the creator's visual reference: a sleepy, deadpan gray-brown tabby cat with triangular ears, half-lidded eyes, ivory muzzle, pinkish brown nose, tucked under an ivory blanket against a warm gray background. Preserve this cat's character and muted colors. Approach 1, mascot: draw an original, exceptionally bold, polished flat illustration of this sleepy tabby head peeking above ONE folded ivory blanket edge, a unified centered mark, broad dark espresso outlines, warm gray and taupe fur, two or three broad tabby stripes only, cream muzzle and blanket. Huge head, clearly separated sleepy eyes; quirky deadpan humor. Full-bleed opaque warm gray-taupe background fills every pixel of the square. Head and blanket form one bold silhouette and fill nearly all the canvas, blanket extending beyond bottom edge. Keep all essential facial features and ears inside a centered circular crop. Must remain crisp and unmistakable as a 32 pixel circular wallet icon. Minimal large shapes. No fine fur, no floral blanket pattern, no scenery. NO words, NO letters, NO numerals, NO lettering, NO symbols, NO rim, NO border, NO frame, NO white margin, NO watermark, NO transparent areas. The provided photo is reference only; reimagine it as professional mascot artwork.

## Option 2

Use case: logo-brand. One 1024x1024 square opaque token logo, approach 2: a radically minimal geometric symbol. The reference photo is the creator's sleepy gray-brown tabby cat tucked into an ivory blanket; preserve that character and those colors but reduce it to an expertly designed icon. One huge unified cat-head silhouette built from a rounded taupe shape and two triangular ears, centered, with TWO thick dark espresso horizontal sleepy eyelids, a single broad ivory muzzle shape and a tiny dusty rose triangular nose; only two broad tabby forehead bars. A single ivory folded blanket shape crosses the bottom quarter. Hard crisp clean shapes, absolutely flat color, very bold visual hierarchy. Background is solid warm medium gray, opaque and fills the whole square with no rim, no border, no margin. Cat head occupies 85 percent width and all key facial features fit a circular wallet crop. Think 32-pixel application icon, readable in silhouette, extreme economy of shapes. NO words, letters, digits, text, decorative glyphs, whiskers, fur texture, small details, gradients, shadow effects, sparkles, frame, watermark or transparency. This option should be much more abstract and geometric than a detailed mascot.

## Option 3

Use case: logo-brand. Make exactly one 1024x1024 square opaque GOONCAT token logo, approach 3: a bold lettermark built from ticker GOONCAT. The reference is a sleepy gray-brown tabby cat peeking from an ivory blanket: keep warm taupe, ivory, espresso and warm gray colors and the sleepy cat personality. Create massive custom block typography in two tightly interlocking rows: top row GOON, bottom row CAT, spelling only GOONCAT. G O O N / C A T. Use a compact square composition that fills about 85 percent of the canvas, legible at 64 pixels. The letters are chunky ivory with thick espresso edging on an opaque full-bleed taupe background. Integrate two simple triangular cat ears into the upper outside corners of the word shape and make the two O counters look like half-lidded sleepy eyes using one thick espresso bar in each. A subtle one-piece folded blanket base under CAT, ivory and warm gray, structurally fused to the lettering. Professional bespoke typography, bold flat forms, exceptionally clean and memorable, slightly offbeat. Only exact text GOONCAT, no dollar sign, slogans, extra words or digits. No copyright characters, fine linework, textured noise, frame, border, margin, watermark or transparency. This must be typographic rather than another cat illustration.

## Option 4

Use case: logo-brand. One square 1024x1024 opaque GOONCAT token logo, approach 4: minted coin or emblem badge. Use the attached creator reference of the sleepy gray-brown tabby cat under an ivory blanket; preserve its half-lidded deadpan expression, pointed ears, cream muzzle and the neutral palette. Design a highly polished antique pewter and warm taupe medallion filling nearly the entire square, with a LARGE sculpted bas-relief sleepy tabby head tucked into a SINGLE ivory folded blanket motif filling the central coin face. Wide shallow embossed shapes, dark espresso recessed eyes and tabby markings, ivory highlights, broad smooth metallic rim. Bold readable relief, no tiny ornaments or microtexture. This is a modern minted emblem with a frontal orthographic view, not a photographed coin on a tabletop. Warm deep gray opaque background fills all square corners. Central cat dominates, remains unmistakable at 64 pixels. No words, NO letters, NO numbers, NO currency glyphs, NO fake inscriptions, NO laurel wreath, NO stars, NO props, NO watermark. Only the single sleepy-cat medallion. Keep reference colors of taupe gray, ivory and dark brown; no gold, neon or saturated colors.

## Option 5

Use case: logo-brand. Generate exactly one 1024x1024 square opaque GOONCAT logo, approach 5: LOUD expressive illustrative meme style. The reference is the creator's sleepy gray-brown tabby cat tucked into an ivory blanket with a deadpan expression. Reimagine that same cat in a punchy original comic illustration: huge angular tabby head tilted slightly, hilariously exhausted half-lidded eyes with heavy dark lids, small pink-brown nose, ivory muzzle, wildly splayed triangular ears, cheeks squashed adorably above a big ivory blanket pulled up under the chin. Thick espresso ink contour, dramatic chunky gray-brown tabby stripes, flat ivory and taupe fills, high-contrast cel shading. Background: bold warm-gray and ivory comic impact rays radiating behind the ONE central cat, no separate objects. Cat dominates 90 percent of canvas; eyes and expression read instantly at 64px. Full bleed with blanket extending beyond bottom and side edges. Preserve the muted reference palette, no neon colors. One character only, no scene or props. NO text of any kind, no letters, digits, speech bubbles, captions, watermarks, frame, border, margins or transparency. Bold professional meme mascot illustration, not a photo, not a realistic painting, no fine fur or small texture.
